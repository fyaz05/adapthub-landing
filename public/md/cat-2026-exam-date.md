# CAT 2026 exam date & timeline

**URL:** https://adapthub.in/cat-2026-exam-date/  
**Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Direct answer

CAT 2026 will be conducted on **Sunday, 29 November 2026** as a computer-based test in **three sessions** across roughly **170 test cities**, per the official CAT 2026 notification released in late July 2026 on iimcat.ac.in.

## Official timeline (CAT 2026 notification)

- Notification: released late July 2026  
- Registration: 3 August 2026 (10:00 AM) – 15 September 2026 (5:00 PM)  
- Fee: ₹2,700 (General/EWS/NC-OBC); ₹1,350 (SC/ST/PwBD)  
- Admit card: 4 November 2026  
- Exam: Sunday, 29 November 2026 (three sessions)  
- Result: expected in the first week of January 2027, per the notification  

## Pattern

- 68 questions, 204 marks, 120 minutes (40 minutes per section, no switching)  
- VARC 24, DILR 22, QA 22  
- +3 correct; −1 wrong MCQ; TITA carries no negative marking
