## What AdaptHub does

- **Adaptive Diagnostic Calibration (ZPD Algorithm):** A 30–45 minute diagnostic finds your baseline, then difficulty is recalibrated per answer to hold the 70–85% accuracy growth band.
- **Two-tier AI hints:** Tier 1 gives a strategic nudge; Tier 2 reveals the full explanation. Hint use freezes level progress for that attempt.
- **Score telemetry:** Learning velocity, weighted accuracy, trend detection, and 7-day rolling performance metrics — not vanity mock scores.
- **Spaced Repetition Queue (SRS):** Memory-decay prevention with adaptive review scheduling.
- **Practice Streaks:** Session-consistency scoring based on accuracy (a calibrated challenge range) and duration (15+ min).
- **Error classification:** Classifies errors into trap-identification and calculation-error categories.
